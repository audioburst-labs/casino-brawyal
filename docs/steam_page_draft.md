# Casino Brawyal: Steam page draft (phase 0)

A working draft for the store page, written so wishlists can start accumulating as early as
possible. Everything here is a proposal for the designer to edit. Numbers in brackets are what
the page needs from production before it can go live.

## Name and tagline

**Casino Brawyal**

Tagline options (one line under the capsule):

1. *A slot machine that pays out in violence.*
2. *Spin. Socket. Strike. A roguelike battler where the reels deal your hand.*
3. *The house always wins. Ace is here to collect.*

Recommendation: option 2 on the page (it names the loop), option 1 in the trailer.

## Short description (300 characters max)

Spin the house's own slot machine for chips, socket them into Ace's abilities, and cut the
casino's staff down to size. A roguelike battler where the reels deal your hand every turn,
every card you own can be gold-plated, and the manager has a loan shark on call.

(286 characters.)

## About this game

**Every turn starts with a spin.** The machine pays chips in four suits. Drag them into the
sockets on Ace's cards: the moment a card's last socket fills it fires, at the enemy you aimed at.
Chips left in the drawer are gone at the end of the turn, so every spin is a hand to be played
out, not a resource to hoard.

**Build the machine, not just the deck.** Buy reels. Sticker a reel's faces so it pays the suits
your cards want. Upgrade a card to Silver, then Gold, and watch its text change. The slot
machine is the deck, and you are allowed to rig it.

**Mark them, then Cash In.** Ace's kit is built around marking a target and cashing the mark in
for a bigger payoff, around Weak enemies who hit for less, around chips he earns back for playing
cards. The synergies are printed on the cards, and the cards glow when the board is set up for them.

**The staff hit back.** Bouncers, Servers who spill drinks that leave you Frail, a Dealer who busts
at twenty-one, a Chip Golem that sheds chips as it breaks, a Loan Shark who offers you a deal every
third round and collects on it, and the manager, Mr. Moneybags, who summons help and heals his crew.
Every enemy's next move is shown above its head before you spin.

**Ten encounters, six paths, one boss.** Fights, elites, a shop, a rest, story encounters with
choices that can start a fight, and a casino inside the casino: a free spin, a dice game, or a
relic hidden among gold.

## Feature bullets

- A slot machine that deals your hand: spin, socket, strike, every turn.
- 25 abilities with Silver and Gold tiers, [target: 45+ at launch].
- Buy reels and sticker their faces to rig the odds in your favour.
- Mark and Cash In, Weak control, chip-earning engines: three ways to build Ace, [target: five].
- Seven enemies with telegraphed intents, two elites, and a boss who brings friends, [target: three acts].
- Loans: a deal every third round from the one enemy who always collects.
- Undo any turn before you pass. Speed up combat to 3x. Every keyword explained on hover.
- Every spin is seeded: the same seed replays the same run.

## Early Access text (if that route is chosen)

**Why Early Access?** The spin-socket-strike loop is finished and tested; what the game needs
now is players telling us which cards and enemies make it sing before we build acts two and three
around them.

**How long?** About nine months.

**How will the full version differ?** Three acts, three heroes, twenty heat levels, controller
and Steam Deck support, more languages.

**Current state?** One hero, one act of ten encounters with six paths, 25 abilities with two
upgrade tiers each, seven enemies, two elites, one boss, a shop, a casino, loans and story
encounters. Music and sound. Fifteen to twenty-five minutes per run.

**Will the price change?** [decide at launch from the wishlist count; see the status review]

**How will the community be involved?** Anonymous play data (opt-out in Settings) and a
Discord/Steam forum for the weekly balance notes.
